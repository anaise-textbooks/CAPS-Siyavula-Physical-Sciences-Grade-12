# Chapter 3: Vertical projectile motion in one dimension

## 3.1 Introduction (ESCJV)

In this chapter we are going to look at the motion of objects that are either projected, thrown, or shot
                  directly into the air, be it vertically upwards, downwards or when objects are dropped. This is
                  something that everyone has experienced while playing games and we all have a strong intuition that
                  'what goes up, must come down', but now we'll study why that is the case.

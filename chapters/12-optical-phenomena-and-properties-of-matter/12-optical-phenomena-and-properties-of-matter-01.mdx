## 12.1 Introduction (ESCQH)

                  water or to supply electricity. Have you ever noticed solar panels on homes and buildings? Have you
                  ever wondered how solar energy is converted to electrical energy? In this chapter, we examine the
                  process that is used to achieve this energy conversion.

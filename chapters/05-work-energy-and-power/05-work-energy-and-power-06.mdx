- **Kinetic energy**: \({E}_{k}=\frac{1}{2}m{v}^{2}\)
- **Conservation of Energy**:
            Energy is never created nor destroyed, but is only transformed from
            one form to another.
- **Conservation of Mechanical Energy**:
            in the absence of non-conservative forces mechanical energy is conserved.
- When a force acting on an object has a component along the line of motion, work is done.
- Work is the process of transferring energy from object or system to another.
- Energy is the ability to do work.
